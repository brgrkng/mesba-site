Metavision

An observer that never blinks.

Metavision is an automated observer for Dota 2 broadcasts. It reads the live match, decides where the story is, frames every hero in the fight and moves the camera the way a professional observer would. The moment a human touches the controls it hands over, and it picks up again when they let go. The name comes from Isagi's Metavision in Blue Lock: seeing the whole field at once.

Reads the game

Valve's Game State Integration streams every hero, item, building and Roshan about once a second. Retail Dota has no remote console, so commands go in through config files and simulated input.

Finds the story

Detectors flag fights, ganks and objectives. A director ranks them, holds each shot long enough to read, and rotates through heroes when it's quiet.

Gets there first

It watches for smokes, teleports and blink initiations, so the camera is in place before the first hit lands.

Frames the fight

Centres on the action and zooms out notch by notch until every hero in it fits, in PimpmuckL's pimpcasting style.

Runs the show

Pre-game shots, a lane tour at the start, spectator stats like net worth and buyback, Showcase View during pauses, and instant replays through OBS.

Hands over

The moment the human observer moves, it steps back. When they stop, it picks up again.
